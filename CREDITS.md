## Music (placeholder tracks)

Milestone 9 ships the game with real, working placeholder music so it has
a full score immediately — see [`docs/assets/music/TRACKS.md`](docs/assets/music/TRACKS.md)
for which track plays where, and how to swap any of them for your own.

All 8 placeholder tracks are by **Kevin MacLeod** (incompetech.com),
licensed under **Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0**
(http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- "Adventures in Adventureland" — used as `title-theme.mp3`
- "Investigations" — used as `barnett-hall.mp3`
- "Ancient Mystery Waltz Allegro" — used as `mystery-basement.mp3`
- "Sardana" — used as `lisbon.mp3`
- "Ancient Rite" — used as `malta.mp3`
- "Evening" — used as `donana.mp3`

## Sound effects

UI chimes and puzzle sound effects are synthesized at runtime with
[Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/) (MIT license) — no external audio
files.
